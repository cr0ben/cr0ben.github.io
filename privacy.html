AI HQ privacy policy

AI HQ is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It is not available to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, AI HQ reads, labels and sends email in the owner's Gmail account, and reads and changes events in the owner's Google Calendar.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the owner's requests, such as sorting the inbox, finding an email or drafting a reply. Sign-in tokens and working copies of this data are kept on the owner's private server.

Email and calendar content may be sent to third-party AI services so they can carry out those requests. Beyond that, it is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

The owner can withdraw access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
